# Ordering & Fee Collection

**Phase:** 🟡 Phase 2

## Overview

The ordering system is a **C-to-B (Consumer to Business)** fee collection platform. It is NOT a member-to-member marketplace. Willen collects payments for its services and on behalf of partner organisations.

## Fee Types

| Type | Description | Who Can Pay |
|---|---|---|
| **OSS Support/Maintenance Fee** | Payment for open source project support and maintenance | Members, External individuals, External organisations |
| **Software Agent Fee Collection** | Willen acts as agent collecting software fees on behalf of partner organisations | Members, External individuals, External organisations |
| **Donations** | Direct donations to Willen from any source | Members, External individuals, External organisations |

## Payment Channels

| Rule | Region | Currency | Methods | Provider |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **a** | Domestic | JPY | Visa, Mastercard | Pay.jp |
| **b** | Domestic | JPY | JCB, Amex, Bank transfer (automated + manual), Convenience store | Omise + GMO Aozora |
| **c** | International | USD / EUR | International cards | Stripe |

## Subscriptions & Recurring Payments

| Type | Frequency | Refund Policy | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual Membership Fee | Yearly | ❌ No refund once collected | Standard for all active members |
| OSS Support/Maintenance Fee | Monthly or Yearly | TBD | Provisional — details TBC |
| Recurring Donations | Monthly / Yearly / Custom | TBD | Flexible donor-configurable frequency |
