## Activities

Willen operates across three main fields:

=== "Development (開発分野)"
    Creating opportunities for beginner engineers through volunteer projects, sharing development knowledge, and building environments to prevent dropout through mentor support.

=== "Operations (利用・運用分野)"
    IT consulting for corporations and individuals, with support for arts, healthcare, and other sectors. Working toward a society where everyone can use information technology.

=== "Academic (学術分野)"
    Supporting academic presentations in information science and social science, and facilitating business participation through research application. Supporting student entrepreneurship.
