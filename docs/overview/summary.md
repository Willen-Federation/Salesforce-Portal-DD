# Project Summary

## Overview

The **Willen Member Portal** is a comprehensive digital platform built on **Salesforce Nonprofit Edition**, designed to serve as the central hub for all organisational operations of 特定非営利活動法人Willen.

The platform consolidates member management, HR operations, payments, events, notifications, and third-party service integrations into a single system, replacing fragmented manual processes.

## Goals

- **Centralise** all member data and operations in Salesforce
- **Automate** onboarding, approvals, notifications, and payment collection
- **Integrate** with existing tools (Okta, Slack, Microsoft 365)
- **Enable** international donations and contributions via multi-currency payments
- **Provide** a modern, bilingual (JP/EN) portal experience for members and external users

## Two Interfaces

| Interface | Brand | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal Portal** | Willen | Current members and Willen Committee |
| **External Portal** | Willen Federation | New applicants, donors, international sponsors, OSS contributors |

## Platform

The portal is built using:

- **Salesforce Experience Cloud** (Build Your Own — fully custom)
- **Apex** for server-side business logic
- **Lightning Web Components (LWC)** for custom UI
- **Salesforce Nonprofit Edition** as the core CRM and data platform

## Current Domain

- Temporary: `willen.mysite.com`
- Target: TBD (e.g. `portal.willen.jp`)

## Development

This is a **solo developer project** with no fixed deadline. Quality and correctness are prioritised over delivery speed. The approach is **MVP first**, then iterative phases.
